# 🌍 CommunityAction — Crowdfunded Community Action Platform

> **Small Actions. Real Neighbors. Transforming Our Communities.**

CommunityAction is a community-driven crowdfunding and volunteering platform that helps citizens turn local problems into meaningful community projects.

The platform allows people to **identify local problems, create community projects, raise funds, recruit volunteers, and track progress** — all through a single digital platform.

🔗 **Live Demo:**  
https://crowdfunded-community-action-platform.ai.studio/

💻 **GitHub Repository:**

## 🚀 About the Project

Many local community problems are visible but remain unresolved because there is no simple way for citizens to organize resources and people around them.

For example:

- 🏞️ A local park needs cleaning
- 📚 A school needs books
- 💡 A street needs better lighting
- 🧹 A neighborhood needs a cleanliness drive
- 🌱 A community wants to create a small green space

**CommunityAction** provides a digital marketplace where citizens can post these problems as projects and allow other members of the community to contribute through:

- 💰 Funding
- 🙋 Volunteering
- 📍 Location-based discovery
- 📊 Progress tracking

The goal is to transform **community problems into community-driven solutions.**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏗️ Create Community Projects

Citizens can create projects describing a problem in their local area and propose a solution.

Each project can contain information such as:

- Project title
- Description
- Category
- Funding goal
- Location
- Project image
- Volunteer requirements
- Project status

---

### 💰 Crowdfunding

Community members can financially support projects they care about.

The platform includes:

- Funding goals
- Amount raised
- Funding progress
- Contributor information
- Mock payment flow

> ⚠️ The payment functionality is intended for demonstration/testing purposes and does not process real financial transactions.

---

### 🙋 Volunteer Registration

Not every contribution needs to be financial.

Users can participate by volunteering their:

- Time
- Skills
- Effort
- Local knowledge

Projects can therefore receive both **financial and human support**.

---

### 📊 Live Progress Tracking

Projects provide visual progress indicators showing how close they are to achieving their funding goals.

This makes the project's progress transparent and easy to understand.

---

### 🗺️ Interactive Map View

Community projects can be explored geographically through an interactive map.

This helps users discover:

- Nearby projects
- Areas requiring support
- Community initiatives
- Local opportunities to volunteer or contribute

---

### 👤 User Profiles

Users can interact with the platform through individual profiles.

Profiles help create a more transparent community environment by connecting users with the projects and contributions they participate in.

---

### 🔍 Explore Projects

Users can browse community projects and discover initiatives based on their interests and location.

The platform is designed to make finding meaningful local opportunities simple and engaging.

---

## 🧠 Why This Project?

CommunityAction combines **social impact + technology + community participation**.

Instead of building another generic CRUD application, the project focuses on a real-world problem:

> **How can technology make it easier for ordinary citizens to collectively solve problems in their own communities?**

The project demonstrates concepts such as:

- Full-stack web development
- CRUD operations
- REST API architecture
- User interaction
- Crowdfunding workflows
- Volunteer management
- Location-based discovery
- Progress tracking
- AI-assisted development
